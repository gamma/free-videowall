Your USB stick. Your playlist. Just play.

Free Videowall plays your own videos and pictures in a repeating loop. No menus to navigate. No ads, subscriptions, in-app purchases or paid upgrades. All features are free. No account inside the app.

Copy your files onto a compatible USB stick and put playlist.m3u in its root folder. An M3U is simply a text file listing what to play. Files can live in subfolders. Mix videos and pictures, choose their order and set how long each picture stays on screen.

Optional 1.5-second picture blends also work from the last item back to the first. Prepare small start and end images for videos with the free computer helper. Transitions blend still frames; audio is not crossfaded.

Use the remote to pause, resume or seek within a video. Playback begins when you open the app. A device with USB media support and compatible files is required; automatic launch when the TV powers on is not guaranteed.
